Free play credits only. No deposits, no cash-out, no prizes. Your win chance each round is 30% (3 in 10). Stake 10, win returns 20.

Rules and odds

You start with 100 play credits. Each round costs 10. A win returns 20, so a win is a net gain of 10.

Your win chance is 30% every round (3 outcomes in 10 win). The result is drawn by your browser's secure random generator. The probe flight is only animation. Which planet you pick and how the probe flies do not change the odds: 3 of the 10 planets are beacon worlds, drawn at random each round.

Over many rounds you get back about 6 credits for every 10 you stake, so credits tend to go down. That is how the numbers are set, and it is why this is for fun only.

Play credits have no value. They cannot be bought, withdrawn, redeemed or traded. Nothing here is real money or a prize. A short break note appears every 25 rounds.
