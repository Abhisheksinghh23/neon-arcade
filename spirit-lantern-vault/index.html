Rules and odds

You start with 100 play credits. Each round costs 10. A win returns 20 credits in total (your 10 stake plus 10 more), a net gain of 10. A loss costs the 10 stake.

Your win chance is 30% every round (3 outcomes in 10 win). The result is drawn by your browser's secure random generator. Which lantern you pick does not change the odds. The page says so because it is true: the 3 spirit lanterns are drawn at random each round.

Over many rounds you get back about 6 credits for every 10 you stake, so credits tend to go down. That is how the numbers are set, and it is why this is for fun only.

Play credits have no value. They cannot be bought, withdrawn, redeemed or traded. Nothing here is real money or a prize. A short break note appears every 25 rounds.
