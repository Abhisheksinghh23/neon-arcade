Difficulty sets how many moves ahead the Forge looks. Free skill game, nothing to win or spend.

How to play

Take turns placing a rune on the glowing 5x5 board. The center anvil is blocked. You and the Forge each place 12 runes.

When your new rune makes a straight line of 3 or more of your runes (across, down or diagonal), you score. A line of 3 scores 1, a line of 4 scores 2, and a line of 5 scores 3. Extending a line scores again, so chains build fast.

When the board is full, the higher score wins. The duel alternates who starts. It is pure tactics, with no luck and no credits. Apprentice looks 1 move ahead and sometimes plays loosely, Smith looks 2 moves ahead, Master looks 4.
